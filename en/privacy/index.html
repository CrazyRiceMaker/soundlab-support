SoundLab privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026

Who provides the app

Chen Chen provides SoundLab. For support and privacy requests, email chrischen.piggy@gmail.com.

Speed and location

GPS mode uses your device's current speed and accuracy to control simulated audio. The app extracts speed, accuracy and status from the system location update and discards the position. It does not record, persist or upload coordinates or routes. Speed is used in memory; no driving route is created. Manual mode does not need location permission. You can deny or revoke location access in system settings.

Background audio and location are used only for an active driving session after you choose to keep running when locked. This option is off by default. Otherwise the session stops when the app enters the background. You can stop a session in the app or through its system controls. The operating system may also stop it.

Saved data and iCloud

Favorites, recent sound selections, settings and aggregate session duration are saved on the device. They do not contain coordinates. When iCloud is available, the app can synchronize these records through your private iCloud database. We do not operate a server that receives this data. Apple operates the iCloud service under its own terms and privacy policy.

The app uses an opaque local account key to keep different iCloud accounts' local stores separate. Switching accounts does not automatically merge the previous account's records into the new account. Previously saved files can remain on that device. iCloud availability does not prove that every device has completed synchronization.

Purchases

Apple handles payments and purchase verification. The app reads verified transactions to determine access to sounds; it does not receive your full payment-card details. An app-specific random purchase token may be stored in Apple's iCloud key-value service and sent to Apple with a purchase. It is not an unlock credential. Purchase access follows the Apple account; it is separate from iCloud data synchronization.

Tracking and diagnostics

There are no ads, tracking domains or third-party analytics SDKs. The app does not sell personal information or use it for cross-app tracking. No developer-operated analytics upload is implemented. If you choose to export data or share information with support, review the file and recipient before sending it. You control that sharing action.

Your choices

You can change location access in system settings, use Manual mode, disable locked-screen operation, export saved app data and request deletion through the app. Cloud deletion requires confirmation and a valid current account; if the cloud request fails, local data is retained and the app reports failure. Deleting app records does not cancel purchases or remove copies you exported, other devices' unsynchronized copies, or system backups. Manage those copies and Apple's services separately where applicable.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or obtain a copy of personal data, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, and contact a supervisory authority. Mandatory rights under applicable law are not limited by this policy. Email chrischen.piggy@gmail.com for requests you cannot handle through the app or Apple's settings.

Children and changes

The app is not designed specifically for children under 13. It must not be used by a driver to interact with the phone while moving. Material changes to data handling require an updated policy and, where required, additional notice or consent.
